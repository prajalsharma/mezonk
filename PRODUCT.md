# MEZONK — product context

register: product (the landing page is the one brand-register surface)

**What it is.** An independent, non-custodial binary prediction market on Mezo. People buy YES or NO shares with MUSD, can sell before close, and redeem after an outcome is final. Every market carries structured, hash-committed resolution rules and a public proposal → challenge → arbitration trail.

**Who uses it, and where.** Bitcoin-holding DeFi users who already borrow MUSD on Mezo. Mostly on phones in the evening scrolling markets, then on a laptop when creating a market or checking a dispute. Both lighting conditions are real, so the product ships light and dark themes that follow the system and can be toggled.

**Tone.** Plain, exact, unhyped. A financial tool, not a casino. Short labels, sentence case, no jargon without an inline explanation. Never imply guaranteed profit.

**Brand.** MEZONK wordmark: Hanken Grotesk 600, caps, +7% tracking. Mark: an "M" whose valley is a chart line, with a red marker dot. Palette: cream #F2EFE9, ink #14110F, red #FF004D. Red is rare: logo marker, probability marker, NO side, destructive actions.

**Anti-references.** Purple Web3 gradients, glass cards, neon, casino confetti, fake activity tickers, hero metrics, eyebrow labels on every section.
