Once your wallet has a Mezo loan, the *Borrow* page shows *Your Mezo loan* instead of the open form: *BTC locked*,
*You owe*, *Ratio* and *Liquidation at*, then five tabs: *Repay*, *Borrow*, *Add BTC*, *Take out* and *Close*. Each
tab shows the loan after the change before you sign, using the same rules Mezo applies, so a change that Mezo would
reject is explained instead of failing in your wallet.

## What each tab does

| Tab | What happens | Allowed in recovery mode |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *Repay* | Pays back part of the loan from this wallet's MUSD | Yes |
| *Borrow* | Borrows more MUSD on the same loan | No |
| *Add BTC* | Locks more BTC, raising the ratio | Yes |
| *Take out* | Unlocks some BTC, lowering the ratio | No |
| *Close* | Pays everything back and unlocks all BTC | No |

Recovery mode is when Mezo's whole system is under 150%. Mezo then only accepts changes that make loans safer, so
the app shows a notice and disables the other tabs' buttons.

## The limits, in plain words

- **You can't repay down to zero and keep the loan open.** Mezo keeps every open loan at or above its minimum size.
  To pay off the rest, close the loan.
- **Closing needs less MUSD than *You owe*,** because Mezo's 200 MUSD deposit is burned back instead of being paid
  by you.
- **Borrowing more is capped** by the limit Mezo set when the loan opened, and the loan must stay at 110% or more.
- **Taking BTC out** must keep your loan at 110% or more and the whole system at 150% or more.

## A worked example

A loan that owes 2,202 MUSD (2,000 received, 2 fee, 200 deposit), with Mezo testnet's minimum net debt of 1,800 MUSD
on 2026-11-06 and no interest accrued yet:

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Most you can repay and keep the loan open | 2,202 − 200 − 1,800 = **202 MUSD** |
| MUSD needed to close | 2,202 − 200 = **2,002 MUSD** |

So with 500 MUSD of winnings you could repay 202 MUSD now, or wait until you have 2,002 MUSD and close. Interest
accrues on top over time; *You owe* always includes it.

## How it works exactly

All five tabs send one transaction from your wallet to Mezo's `BorrowerOperations`. Before the wallet prompt the app
re-reads the price, parameters and your loan at the latest block, re-runs the check, finds sorted-list hints and
estimates gas (see [Borrowing](/docs/mezo/borrowing#right-before-the-wallet-prompt)). Your debt is read with
`TroveManager.getEntireDebtAndColl(you)`, adding principal, interest and any pending redistribution.

### Repay: `repayMUSD(amount, upperHint, lowerHint)`

```
maxRepay = debt − MUSD_GAS_COMPENSATION − minNetDebt
```

- Mezo requires `debt − gasCompensation − amount ≥ minNetDebt`, and the amount can never exceed
  `debt − gasCompensation`. The app enforces the first, which is the tighter one.
- Mezo checks your MUSD balance and burns the repaid MUSD from the caller (your wallet). Interest is paid first, then
  principal.
- If your loan is already at the minimum size, *maxRepay* is zero and the tab tells you to close instead.
- *Max* fills the smaller of *maxRepay* and your MUSD balance.

### Borrow more: `withdrawMUSD(amount, upperHint, lowerHint)`

- A fee of `amount × borrowingRate` is added to the debt (none in recovery mode, but the app blocks this tab there).
- New total debt must stay at or below `getTroveMaxBorrowingCapacity(you)`, fixed when the loan opened as the debt
  that would put it at 110% at the opening price.
- The new ratio must be at least `MCR` (110%), and Mezo also requires the system ratio to stay at or above `CCR`.
- In recovery mode Mezo only allows a debt increase together with added BTC that leaves the loan at 150% or more and
  higher than before. Borrowing alone always lowers the ratio, so it is blocked.
- The quick fill *Up to 200%* borrows only as much as keeps the loan at 200%.

### Add BTC: `addColl(upperHint, lowerHint)` payable

BTC goes in as `msg.value`. Always allowed: it can only raise the ratio. The app keeps 0.001 BTC back for gas when
you tap *Max*.

### Take out BTC: `withdrawColl(amount, upperHint, lowerHint)`

- Blocked in recovery mode (Mezo: `Collateral withdrawal not permitted Recovery Mode`).
- The loan's new ratio must be at least 110%.
- The system ratio after the withdrawal must stay at or above 150%:
  `(systemColl − amount) × price / systemDebt ≥ CCR`. The app reads `getEntireSystemColl` and
  `getEntireSystemDebt` at the same block as the price to check this.
- Withdrawing all the BTC isn't possible; close the loan instead.
- The quick fill *Keep 200%* takes out as much as leaves the loan at 200%.

### Close: `closeTrove()`

```
closeCost = debt − MUSD_GAS_COMPENSATION
```

- Your wallet must hold at least `closeCost` MUSD. Mezo burns that from your wallet and burns the 200 MUSD deposit
  from its gas pool.
- Blocked in recovery mode.
- Mezo also rejects a close that would take the system ratio under 150%. The app checks
  `(systemColl − yourColl) × price / (systemDebt − yourDebt) ≥ CCR` first.
- All your BTC returns to your wallet in the same transaction. The button reads *Repay … MUSD and close*.

### Price moves between seeing and signing

For *Borrow* and *Take out*, the app passes the ratio you saw minus 2% (relative) as a floor. If the fresh read at
signing time puts the loan below it, the app stops with "The BTC price moved and this loan's ratio dropped" instead
of sending.
