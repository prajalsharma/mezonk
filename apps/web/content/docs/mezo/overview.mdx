MEZONK is a prediction market that only makes sense on Mezo. Every market is priced and paid in MUSD, Mezo's
bitcoin-backed dollar. If you hold BTC instead of MUSD, the app opens a Mezo loan for you from your own wallet, and
BTC price markets are designed to settle from the same BTC price Mezo uses for its own loans. This page lists every
one of those touchpoints in one place.

## The short version

- **MUSD is the only money.** You pay for shares in MUSD, winners are paid in MUSD, bonds and fees are in MUSD. There
  is no other token and no MEZONK token.
- **You can borrow MUSD inside the app.** Lock BTC, get MUSD, trade. The loan is a normal Mezo loan, signed by your
  wallet against Mezo's contracts. MEZONK never holds your BTC or your loan.
- **The trade ticket can borrow for you.** If a trade is short on MUSD, it offers to borrow exactly the difference
  from your Mezo loan, and the claim screen can send winnings back to the loan.
- **BTC price markets read Mezo's PriceFeed.** A market like "BTC at or above $100,000 when trading closes" settles
  from the price Mezo uses for loans and liquidations. This part is built and tested but not deployed yet.

## Why it matters for MUSD

A prediction market is a place where people want to hold dollars for days or weeks: to back a side, to seed a new
market's order book, or to post a bond on a result. On MEZONK every one of those dollars is MUSD. Each MUSD in a
market was either bought or borrowed against BTC on Mezo, so activity on MEZONK turns directly into MUSD demand and,
through in-app borrowing, into new Mezo loans.

The loop the app is built around:

1. You hold BTC and want to back a side without selling it.
2. You open a Mezo loan from the app. Mezo mints MUSD to your wallet; your BTC stays locked in Mezo's contracts.
3. You trade with that MUSD. It sits in MEZONK's exchange as collateral for your shares.
4. You win, claim MUSD, and repay the loan from the winnings. Your BTC unlocks.

See [Bet from your loan](/docs/mezo/bet-from-your-loan) for the exact flow.

## How it works exactly

### Touchpoint 1: MUSD as the only collateral

| | Testnet (31611) | Mainnet (31612) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MUSD | `0x118917a40FAF1CD7a13dB0Ef56C86De7973Ac503` | `0xdD468A1DDc392dcdbEf6db6e34E89AA338F9F186` |

- The exchange takes the collateral token in its constructor and stores it as `immutable collateralToken`. It can
  never be changed. The deploy script refuses to deploy unless the address has code, 18 decimals and the symbol
  `MUSD`.
- Addresses live in two places only: `contracts/src/MezoAddresses.sol` and `apps/web/src/lib/chain/config.ts`. The
  app never resolves a token by its symbol.
- Every `transferFrom` into a MEZONK contract checks the balance before and after. If the amount received differs
  from the amount asked for, the call reverts with `UnsupportedToken`. MUSD is a standard ERC-20, so this never
  triggers, but it means a fee-on-transfer token could not be swapped in.
- One Yes share plus one No share is always backed by exactly 1 MUSD held by the exchange. See
  [Solvency](/docs/security/solvency) for how to check that yourself.

### Touchpoint 2: borrowing through BorrowerOperations

The app calls Mezo's MUSD borrowing contracts ([mezo-org/musd](https://github.com/mezo-org/musd), a Liquity-v1-style
design) straight from your wallet. The addresses come from mezo-org/musd's deployment files and were checked on-chain
on 2026-10-22: every contract has code, they reference each other, and `MUSD.mintList(BorrowerOperations)` is true.

| Contract | Testnet | Mainnet |
| --- | --- | --- |
| BorrowerOperations | `0xCdF7028ceAB81fA0C6971208e83fa7872994beE5` | `0x44b1bac67dDA612a41a58AAf779143B181dEe031` |
| TroveManager | `0xE47c80e8c23f6B4A1aE41c34837a0599D5D16bb0` | `0x94AfB503dBca74aC3E4929BACEeDfCe19B93c193` |
| SortedTroves | `0x722E4D24FD6Ff8b0AC679450F3D91294607268fA` | `0x8C5DB4C62BF29c1C4564390d10c20a47E0b2749f` |
| HintHelpers | `0x4e4cBA3779d56386ED43631b4dCD6d8EacEcBCF6` | `0xD267b3bE2514375A075fd03C3D9CBa6b95317DC3` |
| PriceFeed | `0x86bCF0841622a5dAC14A313a15f96A95421b9366` | `0xc5aC5A8892230E0A3e1c473881A2de7353fFcA88` |
| InterestRateManager | `0xD4D6c36A592A2c5e86035A6bca1d57747a567f37` | `0x4a453700d157717Fe02fB62E7700ED7845048285` |

Writes the app sends to `BorrowerOperations`, always with `msg.sender` = your wallet:

| Function | Used for |
| --- | --- |
| `openTrove(debt, upperHint, lowerHint)` payable | Open a loan; BTC is `msg.value` |
| `withdrawMUSD(amount, upperHint, lowerHint)` | Borrow more, including the trade-ticket shortfall |
| `repayMUSD(amount, upperHint, lowerHint)` | Repay part, including repaying from winnings |
| `addColl(upperHint, lowerHint)` payable | Add BTC |
| `withdrawColl(amount, upperHint, lowerHint)` | Take BTC out |
| `closeTrove()` | Repay everything and unlock all BTC |

Reads, all taken at one block number so price, mode and fees agree with each other (`readBorrowParams` in
`apps/web/src/lib/mezo/borrow.ts`):

| Contract | Reads |
| --- | --- |
| PriceFeed | `fetchPrice()` |
| BorrowerOperations | `minNetDebt()`, `borrowingRate()`, `MUSD_GAS_COMPENSATION()`, `MCR()`, `CCR()` |
| InterestRateManager | `interestRate()` |
| TroveManager | `checkRecoveryMode(price)`, `getEntireSystemColl()`, `getEntireSystemDebt()`, `getTroveStatus(addr)`, `getEntireDebtAndColl(addr)`, `getTroveMaxBorrowingCapacity(addr)` |
| SortedTroves, HintHelpers | `getSize()`, `getApproxHint(...)`, `findInsertPosition(...)` for gas hints |

Details: [Borrowing](/docs/mezo/borrowing) and [Manage your loan](/docs/mezo/manage-your-loan).

### Touchpoint 3: PriceFeed settlement for BTC markets

`MezonkPriceResolver` reads `PriceFeed.fetchPrice()` and the oracle's `latestRoundData()` right after a market
closes, compares the price with a strike recorded before close, and proposes Yes or No through MEZONK's normal
resolution process, with an evidence hash of the exact reading. The proposal can still be challenged. Details and
limits: [BTC price markets](/docs/mezo/btc-price-markets).

<Callout type="warn" title="Next deployment">
  `MezonkPriceResolver` is merged on GitHub and tested against the real exchange and resolution contracts, but it is
  not deployed on testnet yet. No live market settles from the PriceFeed today.
</Callout>

### What MEZONK does not do with Mezo

- It never custodies BTC and never holds or manages anyone's loan. There is no MEZONK contract that calls
  BorrowerOperations.
- It does not use the MEZO token for anything.
- It does not route MUSD through any other protocol.
